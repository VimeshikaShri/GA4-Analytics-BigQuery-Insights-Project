# Analytics Report — July 2026

*Compiled July 30, 2026 from Google Analytics 4 exports covering July 1–28, 2026 (Last 28 days), "All Users" segment.*

## Highlights

- **55 total users**, **56 new users**, and **63 sessions** over the 28-day window — but activity was essentially flat until **July 26**, when nearly all sessions occurred.
- **India** was the top country (24 users, 44%), followed by **Venezuela** (9) and **Pakistan** (5).
- Traffic split almost evenly between **Direct** (51% of sessions) and **Paid Search** (41%).
- **Mobile web** drove 84% of active users; **Chrome** was the dominant browser (71%).
- No revenue, transactions, or key-event conversions were recorded in this period.

## Notes

- Nearly every time-series chart in the source reports is flat until **~July 26, 2026**, then spikes — worth checking whether this reflects a launch, campaign start, or a tracking/tagging change.
- **New users (56) slightly exceeds total users (55)** in several tables, and per-channel totals show the same pattern (e.g. Paid Search: 26 total users vs. 27 new users). This is a normal artifact of how GA4 processes small datasets, not a data error.
- **"(not set)"** rows (Country, Landing page) typically indicate missing referrer or geo signals — often from direct app opens or blocked tracking parameters.
- With only 55 active users, GA4 doesn't yet have enough data to populate age, gender, or interest breakdowns.
- All figures are transcribed from GA4 UI PDF exports; percentages are as shown in the original reports and may not sum to exactly 100% due to rounding.
